I am a Lecturer in Economics above the Bar (equivalent to Assistant Professor) at the University of Galway’s J.E. Cairnes School of Business and Economics. I am also an ESRI Research Affiliate with the Behavioural Research Unit.

Research

My main research interests are behavioural economics, experimental economics, and using behavioural science to understand individual decision-making, particularly in market settings — that is, consumer decision making.

My current research agenda focuses primarily on experimental environmental economics, specifically carbon tax. I am investigating how tax structure, framing, and comprehension influence perceived fairness and acceptability. I began this agenda as an Irish Research Council Postdoctoral Fellow.
