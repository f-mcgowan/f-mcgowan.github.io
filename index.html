I am a behavioural economist and Lecturer in Economics at the University of Galway’s J.E. Cairnes School of Business and Economics, where I am a member of the Centre for Economic Research on Inclusivity and Sustainability and Programme Director of the MSc in Fintech.

I use experimental methods to study how people make decisions and evaluate public policies (particularly related to climate), and to test the effect of policy interventions. I am also an ESRI Research Affiliate with the Behavioural Research Unit, where I previously held an Irish Research Council Fellowship.

Three strands

Climate policy support
Why people back a climate policy or oppose it — perceived effectiveness, perceived fairness, self-interest — and how those judgements shift with the information available. I test how distributional information changes support for carbon taxes, focusing on aviation.
Status quo bias and active travel
Opposition to active travel infrastructure is not only about the infrastructure. I study how status quo bias changes the process by which people form opinions about it.
Intuitive numerical reasoning
Asked to guesstimate the sum of a sequence of numbers, people systematically underestimate the total. This bias is robust across measurement methods and contexts, and helps explain both the undervaluation of energy-efficiency savings and the appeal of paying by subscription.

Funded projects

LE CHÉILE Environmental Protection Agency
Local Examples of Cooperation and Harnessing Experimental Insights to Lower Emissions. Comparative case study analysis and online collective-action experiments, examining how to foster and sustain local climate cooperation — between individuals and between groups — feeding into a practitioner toolkit. Awarded €163,663 under the EPA’s 2025 research call. Principal Investigator.
LETSGO Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland
Designing electric vehicle grant schemes to maximise both effectiveness and equity, combining an international policy review, behavioural survey experiments, and a national microsimulation model. Principal Investigator, with Cathal O’Donoghue.
